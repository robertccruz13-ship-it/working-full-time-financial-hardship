# working-full-time-financial-hardship
SDS capstone examining earnings, housing costs, and financial hardship among young adults who work at least 30 hours a week. 

# Working Full-Time but Still Struggling to Afford Basic Necessities

## Project Overview
This Social Data Science capstone examines the relationship between employment, earnings, housing costs, and financial hardship among young adults in the United States.

The project focuses on individuals ages 18–29 who usually work at least 30 hours per week.

## Research Question
Among young adults ages 18–29 who usually work at least 30 hours per week, how are earnings and housing costs associated with financial hardship in the United States?

## Research Objectives
- Examine household poverty among young adults who work substantial hours.
- Investigate housing-cost burden among eligible renters.
- Explore how financial hardship differs across earnings levels.
- Examine differences by education and geographic location where possible.

## Data Source
The project uses the 2024 American Community Survey Public Use Microdata Sample (ACS PUMS), provided by the U.S. Census Bureau.

[ACS PUMS Data and Documentation](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/microdata/documentation/2024.html)
